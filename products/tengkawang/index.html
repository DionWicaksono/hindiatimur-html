Growing Regions

Sourced from
Borneo's Rainforests

Tengkawang grows exclusively in the primary rainforests of Borneo — one of the oldest and most biodiverse ecosystems on earth. Each region produces Shorea nuts with subtly different fatty acid compositions influenced by microclimate and species variety.

01
West Kalimantan
Kalimantan Barat · Borneo

The largest wild Tengkawang harvesting region, home to multiple Shorea species including S. stenoptera and S. pinanga. Deep primary forest coverage supports large nut yields during masting cycles. Primary source for cosmetic and CBS-grade fat.

02
Central Kalimantan
Kalimantan Tengah · Borneo

Remote interior forests at higher elevation yield Tengkawang with slightly higher stearic acid content. Indigenous Dayak communities here maintain generations-old harvesting knowledge and fair-trade cooperative structures.

03
East Kalimantan
Kalimantan Timur · Borneo

Rich in Shorea beccariana and S. seminis. Proximity to established export infrastructure at Balikpapan port makes East Kalimantan a strong source for container-load orders with competitive lead times and reliable logistics.

From Forest to Export

How We Source & Process

A transparent, traceable supply chain ensuring every shipment carries verifiable zero-deforestation provenance and meets international cosmetic, food, and sustainability standards.

01
Indigenous Harvest

Dayak community gatherers wild-collect fallen Tengkawang nuts from primary forest floor during masting season. No tree felling. GPS-tagged harvest zones documented for each lot.

02
Drying & Primary Processing

Nuts are sun-dried at source to reduce moisture, then cold-pressed or expeller-pressed to yield crude Tengkawang fat. Pressing is done at community-level facilities, maximizing local value addition.

03
Refining & Fractionation

Crude fat is RBD-refined (Refined, Bleached, Deodorized) and optionally fractionated to target melting point for cosmetic or CBS specification. Certificate of Analysis issued for every refined lot.

04
Documentation & Shipment

Full compliance package prepared: COA, fatty acid profile, MSDS, phytosanitary certificate, zero-deforestation declaration, and certificate of origin. FCL and LCL available via Balikpapan, Pontianak, or Surabaya.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Is Tengkawang a legal cocoa butter substitute in my market?
Tengkawang/Illipe fat is approved as a Cocoa Butter Equivalent (CBE) or Cocoa Butter Substitute (CBS) in most global markets including the EU (Directive 2000/36/EC allows up to 5% non-cocoa vegetable fats in chocolate), the UK, and ASEAN. Regulations vary by market and application — we recommend verifying with your local food authority. We provide full technical documentation to support your regulatory submission.
What documents do you provide with each shipment?
Every export shipment includes: Certificate of Analysis (fatty acid profile, FFA, IV, melting point, moisture), MSDS/SDS, Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Origin, and a Zero-Deforestation Declaration with GPS-referenced harvest zone data. Organic certificates are available for select lots upon request.
Why is Tengkawang supply seasonal and limited?
Tengkawang trees fruit on a natural "masting" cycle driven by climatic cues — typically every 3–7 years. This means large-scale nut availability only occurs in certain years, making it a genuinely rare and wild-sourced ingredient. We recommend buyers register interest in advance so we can allocate from the next masting event. Forward contracts and reservations are accepted.
What are the minimum order quantities and lead times?
Minimum order is 500 kg for RBD or fractionated Tengkawang fat. Crude Tengkawang may be available in smaller lots. Container loads (FCL, ~18–20 MT) qualify for best pricing. Lead time is typically 3–6 weeks from order confirmation, subject to the seasonal harvest cycle. Sample lots of 1–5 kg are available for qualified buyers at lab/formulation stage.
How does Tengkawang compare to Shea butter for cosmetics?
Tengkawang (Illipe) has a higher stearic acid content (45–55%) versus Shea (35–45%) and a superior melting point for tropical climates, making it particularly valued for stick formulations, lip care, and products requiring firmness and a non-greasy skin feel. It is also genuinely wild-harvested and forest-positive, providing a compelling sustainability story that is increasingly differentiated from plantation-grown alternatives.
Can you supply finished consumer products for white-label?
Yes. In addition to bulk fat, we offer a finished goods line including zero-paraffin Tengkawang candles, artisanal body butters, and seed cake (for agricultural use) that are handcrafted at source in Kalimantan. White-label and private-label options are available for eco-retailers and consumer brands. Minimum quantities and lead times vary by product — contact us for a finished goods catalog.
