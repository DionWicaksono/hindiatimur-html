Storage & Handling

Whole and ground ambergris: store in an airtight, odour-sealed container away from direct heat and humidity. Stable at room temperature; refrigeration not required. Tinctures: store in a cool, dark location; keep sealed when not in use. Do not store near other fragrance materials — ambergris scent is highly pervasive.

Source Waters

Found in Indonesia's
Richest Seas

Indonesia sits at the centre of global sperm whale migration routes. The warm equatorial waters of the Banda Sea, Timor Sea, and Maluku Straits concentrate some of the world's finest ambergris deposits — specimens that have drifted and aged over years before reaching shore.

01
Banda Sea
Central Maluku · Spice Islands

The deep, warm waters of the Banda Sea — the historic heart of the global spice trade — are among the most productive ambergris source zones in the Eastern Hemisphere. Specimens found here are frequently well-aged, displaying the characteristic grey-white surface and sweet, marine-woody scent.

Pelagic · Open Ocean Drift
02
Maluku Straits
North Maluku · Halmahera

The intricate island topography of the Maluku Straits creates natural collection points for ocean drift material. Ambergris found along these coastlines often includes mid-aged specimens with brown-grey colouration and a notably complex, resinous scent profile.

Coastal Drift · Remote Beaches
03
Timor Sea
East Nusa Tenggara · NTT

The Timor Sea lies along a major sperm whale feeding corridor. Specimens from this region are highly variable in age — ranging from recently deposited black pieces to deeply weathered white ambergris found on remote barrier islands. Among the most sought-after material we source.

How We Source & Export

A rigorous, legally compliant process that ensures every specimen is authentic, traceable, and cleared for international trade before it leaves Indonesia.

01
Coastal Collection

Ambergris is collected exclusively as beach-found material — never hunted. Our network of trusted coastal scouts across the Banda Sea, Maluku, and Timor Sea coastlines notify us when specimens are found.

02
Authentication & Grading

Each specimen is authenticated using the hot-needle test, UV fluorescence, and burn-and-float tests. Pieces are graded by colour, estimated age, ambrein content, and scent profile. GC-MS analysis available on request.

03
CITES Compliance

All specimens are processed through the Indonesian Ministry of Environment and Forestry (KLHK) for CITES compliance documentation and export permitting. No specimen ships without complete legal clearance.

04
Packaging & Shipment

Specimens are individually wrapped, sealed, and packed for international air freight or insured courier. Authentication report, Certificate of Origin, and CITES export permit accompany every consignment.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Is trading ambergris legal?
In most countries, yes — provided it is beach-found (not hunted) and traded with the appropriate CITES documentation. Indonesia permits the export of ambergris as a non-detriment-finding product under CITES Appendix II. Importers should verify the legal status in their destination country: the US, Australia, and India prohibit import; the EU, UK, UAE, Japan, and most other markets permit it with valid permits. We provide full export documentation for every shipment.
How do you authenticate specimens?
Every specimen undergoes a hot-needle test (genuine ambergris melts and releases a distinctive marine-musky scent), UV fluorescence inspection (aged ambergris fluoresces under UV light), and a float test (ambergris floats in salt water). For larger lots or high-value pieces, GC-MS (gas chromatography–mass spectrometry) analysis confirming ambrein and epicoprostanol content is available at cost. Buyers receive a written authentication report with each order.
What documents do you provide with each shipment?
Every export consignment includes: CITES Export Permit (KLHK-issued), Certificate of Origin, Authentication Report (with photos and test results), and a specimen description sheet (weight, colour grade, dimensions, estimated age). GC-MS analysis reports are available on request and at additional cost.
What is the minimum order quantity?
For grey/white perfumery grade, the minimum is 50 g. For black/fresh grade, the minimum is 100 g. Tincture (3% or 10%) is available from 50 ml. Sample pieces of 1–5 g can be arranged for qualified fragrance buyers prior to a commercial order — contact us to discuss.
How is availability managed given the rarity?
Ambergris is a genuinely scarce material — supply is irregular and lot-dependent. We maintain a small curated stock of authenticated specimens and notify established buyers when new material arrives. For buyers with ongoing requirements, we recommend registering your interest so we can prioritise allocation to you. Lead times of 1–4 weeks are typical; larger quantities may require longer sourcing windows.
Can you ship internationally and how is it packaged?
Yes. We ship via DHL, FedEx, or insured air freight to all destinations where importation is legal. Whole specimens are individually wax-wrapped and triple-sealed in odour-proof packaging to prevent scent contamination of other goods in transit. Powder is supplied in sealed amber glass jars. All shipments include full commercial documentation for customs clearance. We do not ship to the US, Australia, or India.
